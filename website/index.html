From buried to ready.

The illustration beside these steps shows the recording being played and its transcript appearing beside it, the transcript scrolling past, one passage being highlighted and scored, a landscape interview frame being cropped to vertical around the guest, captions appearing word by word with a hook line above, and finally four vertical clips lined up above the recording's waveform.

  1. It watches and listens.

    Drop in the recording. Speech is transcribed and timed on your Mac, and a few sampled frames show the model what was on screen. Review the transcript and suggested clips for accuracy.

  2. It finds what stands on its own.

    The model you connect reads the transcript and suggests moments that make sense without the hour around them. Each comes ranked, with its reasons.

  3. It turns wide into tall.

    Each clip is reframed to 9:16, and can follow the speaker's face as they lean, turn and gesture.

  4. It tightens every second.

    Captions timed word by word. Fillers and long pauses cut. A hook line on screen from the first frame, so the first second earns the next one.

  5. Then it does it again.

    One recording becomes a stack of clips, ranked for review. You keep the good ones and export them. Posting stays your call.

  1. Delete a word. The cut follows.

    Edit the clip by editing its transcript. Strike a word and the video, captions and timing move with it. Fillers and long pauses are already marked.

  2. Ranked, with reasons.

    Every suggestion carries a score out of 100 and a short why. Disagree freely; the order is advice, not a verdict.

  3. Captions, layout, hook, brand.

    Caption templates, face-following reframing, hook styles and brand kits sit in one panel. The preview matches the export.

  4. Export, then decide.

    An MP4 with burned-in captions and an SRT file, rendered on your Mac. Where it goes next is up to you.

Install in five steps

  1. Download and extract the ZIP. The complete kit includes the installer, instructions, third-party source material, notices and checksums. Keep the extracted folder intact.
  2. Install the app. Open the DMG and drag Clippie to Applications.
  3. Open it. If macOS blocks the unnotarized app and you trust the sender, follow Apple's per-app Open Anyway instructions. If macOS reports damage or malware, stop and contact the sender. Do not disable system security.
  4. Prepare your setup. In Settings, prepare the required local models, connect your provider, check access and apply a routing preset. Review any warnings.
  5. Make and check your clip. Import a short local video, generate within your allowance, review and edit, then export MP4 and SRT. Play the result outside the app and check speech, captions, framing and meaning.
